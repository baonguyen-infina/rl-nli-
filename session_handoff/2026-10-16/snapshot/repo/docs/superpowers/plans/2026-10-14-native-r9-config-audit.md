Research review,14 October2026:
- [GRPO-trained NLI, revised September2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.18376v3): this already studies instruction-tuned Qwen, rationale-free RL, adversarial generalization and adapter ranks. A3B/2B Vietnamese reproduction alone is insufficient novelty. Longer CoT is an empirical question, not a contribution by itself.
- [Likelihood-based rewards,2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.03979v1): likelihood rewards and a combined supervised answer term already exist; their experiments show that artificially preserving CoT length can hurt performance. Our128-token change is an upper bound only.
- [Off-Context GRPO,July2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.19313v1): training-only hints induce a context mismatch requiring correction. Current native groups affect the loss only: each rollout still sees its own original pair, without a gold-label hint or other group's text. Do not add gold labels to prompts as a rescue.
- [CRAFT,May2026 revision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17305): contrastive latent-space RL for safety alignment exists. Our candidate distinction is conditional assignment over canonical answer scores on human-authored sibling hypotheses, not the generic combination of contrastive learning and GRPO.
- [Entailment-graph debiasing,Findings ACL2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.705/): counterfactual NLI data and bias reduction also have prior work. Shared-class-offset invariance of our critic does not establish removal of hypothesis-only or attestation bias; that requires measured probes.
- [GradCache,2021](https://aclanthology.org/2021.repl4nlp-1.31/): decoupling cross-example loss derivatives from model backpropagation is established prior art. Score-Jacobian replay is our implementation of that principle, not a standalone novelty claim. Cite this older foundational boundary alongside the recent reasoning-RL work.
